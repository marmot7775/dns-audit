# Doc 101: Group the result cards into three areas

## Why
The results page shows every check as one flat list: DMARC, SPF and
DKIM pinned first, then everything else by severity (app.js, the sort
after the scope filter in renderResults). A business owner cannot tell
which cards are about their own outgoing mail and which are about
mail coming in or the domain itself. Grouping the cards into three
plain-language areas gives them that map without reading acronyms.

## What to build
Group the cards under three headings, in this order:

1. Sending your mail
   One line under it: Proves mail from your domain is really from you.
   Cards: DMARC, SPF, DKIM, BIMI.
2. Receiving your mail
   One line under it: Where your mail is delivered and whether it
   arrives encrypted.
   Cards: MX Records, MTA-STS, TLS-RPT, DANE.
3. Your domain's DNS
   One line under it: Protects the records everything else depends on.
   Cards: DNSSEC, CAA, Nameservers, Certificate Transparency.

Before mapping, list every card name the engine can put in
data.checks (grep audit_engine.py, checks_extra.py and
result_transformer.py, and run a full audit). Place any name not
listed above in the area it protects (spoofing and reporting checks
belong under Sending your mail) and state each placement in the
report.

## Rules
- One source of truth: a CHECK_AREAS map in app.js. No second copy
  anywhere.
- Inside Sending your mail, keep DMARC, SPF, DKIM pinned in that
  order. Inside every area, everything else keeps the existing
  SEVERITY_ORDER sort.
- Each heading shows a short status count for its area in plain
  words, for example 2 need attention or All passing. Count with the
  existing statusCounts so the area counts add up to the summary
  grid.
- A scope that leaves an area with no cards does not render that
  area's heading.
- Card ids do not change. Every #check-* anchor, openCard call, the
  roadmap links and the technical summary shortcuts must still land
  on the right card, including when the card sits in a closed state.
- Headings are h3 under the existing h2 results-heading, each area a
  section with aria-labelledby. No new interactive controls; headings
  do not collapse.
- Colors from existing tokens only. tests/test_palette_contrast.py
  must still pass in both themes.
- Site copy: no em dashes, no en dashes as punctuation.
- PDF is out of scope for this doc. Note in the report whether the
  PDF protocol order now disagrees with the page, and how.

## Tests
- A unit test that fails if any card name the engine can emit has no
  entry in CHECK_AREAS.
- A test that area counts sum to the summary grid counts for each
  scope.
- Playwright at 375px and 1280px, dark and light: no horizontal
  scroll, headings render, a #check-dmarc and a #check-dnssec link
  each open and land on their card.
- Full suite passes. Report the before and after test count.

## Finish
- Run the cache-bust loop from CLAUDE.md before committing.
- Work on a new branch and open a PR. Do not merge.
- Report back in one fenced markdown block: branch, commit SHA, test
  count before and after, the full card to area mapping, any card you
  placed that was not listed above, and the PDF order note.
